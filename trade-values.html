How to use this chart

  • Add up each side. Sides within about 5% of each other are a fair deal.
  • Two for one. The side getting the single best player should give more: count the second-best piece on a side at about 85% of its value and a third at 75%.
  • This season is rest-of-season value for redraft leagues: the same number as the Redraft board in Rankings and the Redraft calculator. Keeper & Dynasty is long-term value: the same number the Keeper and Dynasty calculators use.
  • 30-day is how far FantasyCalc's market has moved the player in the last 30 days, on this chart's scale.
  • Matchups are the next three opponents. BYE means the team is off that week.

Values are live from FantasyCalc and Dynasty Dealer and refresh every 15 minutes while this page is open.
