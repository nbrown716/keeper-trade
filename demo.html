The short spec

Rules of the league

  1. One winning moveEvery screen gets one obvious primary action and feedback within 100ms.
  2. Color carries meaningVolt is for winning actions, blue for information, and team colors stay below 20% of a view.
  3. Numbers never jitterUse condensed display type and tabular figures; animate changed values with a count-up.
  4. Motion earns its place160ms micro, 320ms standard, 640ms hero. Stagger lists by 30–50ms and honor reduced motion.
  5. Build for the thumbMobile-first layouts, 44px minimum targets, and essential navigation at the bottom.
  6. Never leave a blank playAsync content gets skeletons; failures degrade gracefully. No spinners, no dead ends.
