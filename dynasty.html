          How this calculator works
          • Live values. Every player is the average of two live markets, Dynasty Dealer and FantasyCalc, each scaled so its top player is 100 before your league's scoring rules are applied, shown on a 10,000-point scale. (A rule that lifts the top player himself, such as 6-point passing touchdowns in Superflex, can take him a little above 10,000.) Values refresh every 15 minutes while this page is open.
          • Your scoring. FantasyCalc is read in your format (PPR, Half or Non-PPR; 1QB or Superflex). Dynasty Dealer publishes one market, and it tracks Superflex PPR (its quarterbacks match FantasyCalc's Superflex values, not its 1QB values), so each of its values is moved by the shift FantasyCalc shows for that player between Superflex and your format. Passing-touchdown points and TE premium, which neither market prices, come from Sleeper's live season projections: a player moves by how much the rule changes his points over a waiver-level replacement at his position, scaled to 60% of that (the strength real TE-premium trade markets show) and capped at −20% to +25%.
          • Rookie picks. 2027 to 2029, rounds 1 to 4, early, mid or late, from the same two markets. The two markets disagree more on picks than on players, which is why the range under each total can be wide.
          • Values in 1sts. One unit is a mid 1st in the next rookie draft. "2.1" reads as "about two mid 1sts".
          • Package adjustment. On each side the best piece counts in full, the 2nd at 85%, the 3rd at 75%, the 4th at 65%, the 5th at 55% and the rest at 50%, because one star is scarcer than the same total spread over several roster spots. This is a model setting, not market data. Turn it off for a straight sum.
          • The call. Within 5% is fair. 5% to 15% leans one way. Above 15% is a clear win for the team giving less. The range is the lowest and highest of the two markets; when the ranges overlap, treat the deal as close.
          • This page does not use keeper costs or the 1,000-point house rule. For keeper trades use the Keeper calculator.
